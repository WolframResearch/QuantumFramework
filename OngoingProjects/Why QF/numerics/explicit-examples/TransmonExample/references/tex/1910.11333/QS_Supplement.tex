\section*{Erratum}
The caption of Figure 4 in the main paper \cite{arute2019} incorrectly states that the error bars in the figure represent both statistical and systematic uncertainty. They represent the statistical uncertainty. See Figure~\ref{fig:stat_and_syst_error} for comparison of both types of uncertainty and discussion in Section~\ref{sec:large_scale_xeb} for details. Note that both types of uncertainty were accounted for in the analysis and all conclusions remain intact.
