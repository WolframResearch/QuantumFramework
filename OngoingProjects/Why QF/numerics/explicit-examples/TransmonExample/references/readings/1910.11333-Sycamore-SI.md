## Findings by parameter

**1Q gate duration.**
- Design example only. sup_architecture.tex:4: "For decoherence-dominated errors, a 0.1% error means a factor of about 1000 between coherence and gate times. For example, a 25 μs coherence time implies a 25 ns gate." This paragraph concerns the two-qubit gate design.
- 2Q gate, chosen value. sup_calib_metro.tex:115: "Gate times of 12 ns are chosen to trade off decoherence (too slow) and leakage to higher states of the qubit (too fast)."

**Gate error from T1, first-order model.** sup_errors.tex:37: "For a gate of length t_g with a qubit decay time T_1, averaging over the Bloch sphere ... gives (to first order) an average error probability e_a = t_g/3T_1 ... Pauli error e_P = t_g/2T_1."

**Pulse shape, DRAG and detuning.**
- Envelope: not stated. DRAG: not mentioned.
- Calibration procedure, sup_calib_metro.tex:57: "Fine tune parameters (qubit resonant frequency, drive power, drive detuning [Chen et al., PRL 116, 020501 (2016)]) for π and π/2 pulses." No value is given.
- Amplitude, sup_calib_metro.tex:55: "Perform power Rabi oscillations to find the drive power that gives a π pulse."
- Gate set: π/2 rotations X^{1/2}, Y^{1/2}, W^{1/2} (sup_circuits.tex:32).
- Measured, sup_calib_metro.tex:164: "π pulse fidelities are somewhat worse than π/2 pulse fidelities, which we attribute to reflections from the imperfect microwave environment."
- Control hardware, sup_control.tex:11, 21: 14-bit DACs at 1 GS/s; ±350 MHz bandwidth around the LO.

**Anharmonicity.** Measured over 53 qubits, sup_calib_metro.tex:282: median −208.0 MHz, mean −208.0, stdev 4.7 MHz. η is "roughly constant" (sup_architecture.tex:17).

**Qubit frequencies.** Measured, sup_calib_metro.tex:278-281; median / mean / stdev:

Operating band: "between 5-7 GHz" (line 32). Tuning excursion: "< 100 MHz" (sup_fabrication.tex:5).

**T1.**
- Measured at idle, sup_calib_metro.tex:285: median 15.54 μs, mean 16.04 μs, stdev 4.00 μs, over 53 qubits (line 305).
- T1 fluctuates in time because of TLS defects (sup_large_scale_XEB_results.tex:241, 244).

**T2, T2*, T2 echo, T_phi.** Not stated. T2 is only named (sup_circuits.tex:221). Dephasing appears only qualitatively:
- sup_architecture.tex:8: operating near the maximum frequency reduces 1/f flux noise.
- sup_calib_metro.tex:237: idle frequencies are chosen "to optimize for dephasing".

- Not stated as a number. The Hamiltonian is "truncated to the qubit levels" (sup_architecture.tex:10).

**Other measured values.**
- Coupler g: from 5 to −40 MHz, about −20 MHz "on" (sup_architecture.tex:6).

- Readout: 1 μs. Active reset: a 10 μs pulse.
