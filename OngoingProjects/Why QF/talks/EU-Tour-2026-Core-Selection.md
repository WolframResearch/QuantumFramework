# EU tour 2026: core selection

33 examples from EU-Tour-2026-Showcase.md that together cover every stop, most of them used at two or more stops. Chosen for a sharp, derived answer and for something only an exact or symbolic computation gives.

## The 33, by theme (stops in brackets)

Open systems and dynamics
1. Two atoms in a shared field: superradiance, subradiance and a dark state (Manchester, IoP, Aegiq)
2. Master equation, Kraus channels and a circuit: one Ramsey fringe (NQCC, Fraunhofer)
3. The quantum Zeno crossover as an exceptional point (Oxford, WIAS)
4. Entanglement sudden death (Oxford, Manchester)

Measurement and estimation
5. A driven qubit under continuous measurement (Riverlane, Imperial)
6. A monitored qubit stays in the Bloch ball: Itô's lemma on the sphere (WIAS)
7. Randomized benchmarking: the decay of sequence fidelity (NQCC, Fraunhofer)
8. GHZ against product probes when every qubit dephases (Oxford)

Circuits and algorithms
9. Teleportation with the classical channel as record wires (IoP, Imperial, Online)
10. Grover search: the success probability, iteration by iteration (IoP, Imperial, Online)
11. Shor's algorithm factors 15 and 21 (Imperial, Online)
12. QAOA on MaxCut: the exact landscape and a certified optimum (Manchester, Imperial, Fraunhofer, Online)
13. One site of an arbitrarily long Ising chain after a quench (NQCC, Fraunhofer)
14. Proving a circuit's phase approximation correct over the reals (Fraunhofer, WIAS)

Error correction
15. Five-qubit code: fifteen single-qubit errors, fifteen distinct syndromes (Riverlane, Imperial)
16. A thousand-qubit GHZ state: entanglement entropy as a rank over F₂ (NQCC, Riverlane, Online)
17. Repetition code: the logical error rate is a polynomial in the flip probability (Riverlane)
18. Maximum-likelihood decoding: exact logical error rates of three codes (Riverlane)
19. Compiling a rotation from H and T gates (Riverlane)

Light
20. A beam splitter with symbolic photon numbers (Aegiq, Manchester, IoP)
21. Hong-Ou-Mandel with partially distinguishable photons (Aegiq)
22. The nonlinear sign gate of Knill, Laflamme and Milburn (Aegiq)
23. Fourier interferometers with one photon per port (Aegiq)
24. Bosons, distinguishable particles and fermions at one beam splitter (Aegiq, IoP, Imperial)
25. A Schrödinger-cat state in phase space (Aegiq, Manchester, IoP)

Entanglement and many-body states
26. GHZ and W: two kinds of three-qubit entanglement (Oxford)
27. Which pure states LOCC can convert: Nielsen's majorization (Oxford)
28. Two qubits have no bound entanglement (Oxford, WIAS)
29. The AKLT ring: from a matrix-product state to an exact ground state (Manchester, Oxford)
30. The entanglement profile of an Ising ring, and the bond dimension it needs (NQCC, Oxford, WIAS)

Hardware and beyond
31. Leakage of a fast π pulse on a transmon (NQCC, Riverlane, Fraunhofer)
32. A GHZ state on an IBM processor: where the lost shots go (NQCC, Fraunhofer, Online)
33. The first Bianchi identity from the Riemann tensor's Young diagram (WIAS)
