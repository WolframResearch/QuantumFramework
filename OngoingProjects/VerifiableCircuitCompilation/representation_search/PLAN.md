# Mathematical representation search

Contracts and measured bottlenecks -> three candidate branches -> exact
identity -> independent finite/reference tests -> reversible cost screen ->
fresh wl-verify -> fresh wl-quality -> a decision.

DQI: cache the entire Forney quotient, including the official position
prefactor, as one polynomial modulo the locator. Test root-conditioned
phase degree and the register/Toffoli cost of setup and erasure.
Second candidate: retain projective locator/evaluator coefficients to avoid
normalization divisions in the classical recurrence. Check exact recurrence
and phase invariance; do not equate a classical inversion-free update with a
reversible quantum cost saving.

Inverse square root: compensate a curvature change by changing the affine
terms, derive its minimax error, then screen new coefficient precisions on
the full official integer domain. Existing certified tables stay unchanged.

DFTHC: test whether free invalid addresses or an output basis change can
reduce the actual address-monomial support, and compare address split arities.
Reject an exhausted representation rather than repeatedly minimizing ANF.

Each branch has at most two candidate refinements. A failed identity returns
to its branch; a cost failure rejects that candidate. A quality code fix
returns to correctness, with at most three correctness visits, three rounds
per correctness visit and three quality rounds. Code deliverable, no notebook.

No circuit implementation, factor fitting, incumbent modification, submission,
commit or push belongs to this stage. Raw tables stay in ignored local/.
