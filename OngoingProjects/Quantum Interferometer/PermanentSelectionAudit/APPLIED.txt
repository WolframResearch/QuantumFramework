Measured storage limitation
  The approved rule does not inspect packed-array storage. Independent seven-
  trial, cache-cleared checks at n=12, m=128, two occupied modes found:
    packed random unitary: new 1.361ms, old 11.834ms (medians);
    unpacked Fourier matrix: new 20.446ms, old 11.953ms;
    explicitly complex-packed Fourier: new 1.332ms, old 12.392ms.
  These are complete normalized amplitudes, excluding startup and matrix
  preparation. Plain ToPackedArray left the mixed real/complex Fourier matrix
  unpacked; the explicit Complex conversion packed both W and its selected block.
  No packing conversion or additional selection condition was added to the
  approved rule. It is a local empirical policy, not an always-fastest guarantee.
