PERMANENT SELECTION PROPOSAL — REVIEW BEFORE KERNEL CHANGE

Historical pre-implementation report. The approved rule has since been applied;
see APPLIED.txt for production test results and implementation limitations.
At the time of this report, the QI kernel and its existing tests were unchanged.
Proposal.wl is an isolated prototype with an optional fresh-kernel, in-memory
integration hook.

Rule
  n = total photons, m = modes.
  Ks = Product[s_j+1]; Kt = Product[t_j+1]; K=min(Ks,Kt).
  r = number of positive output counts in the cheaper orientation:
      Count[t,_?Positive] when Ks<=Kt; otherwise Count[s,_?Positive].
  C = m^2 + K*m*(r+1).
  Choose the existing repeated routine only when
      K < 2^n AND gamma*C < n*2^n.
  gamma=16 for MachinePrecision; gamma=0.1 for other approximate inputs.
  Exact/symbolic matrices continue through built-in Permanent. Vacuum and
  photon-number mismatch remain1 and0. Both algorithms and normalization are
  preserved. Only dispatch changes.
  These are empirical work scores, not assertions about Permanent's internals.
  m^2 approximates whole-W precision conversion; K*m*(r+1) approximates the
  current interpreted sum. The much smaller arbitrary-precision coefficient
  reflects the measured cost of WL's nonmachine Permanent evaluation.

Validation
  Constants selected from training seed1101 and high-precision seed3303.
  Machine seed2202 was held out:132 rows. Proposed selected the faster route
  in all112 cases with both methods completed; it chose direct for all20
  other rows whose repeated branch hit a limit. No guarantee outside this grid.
  Complete-selector validation:40 additional cases on unseen n6/10/14/18,
  modes24/96 and precision35, plus tiny n2/4 free cases. All40 proposed calls
  completed;4 original calls hit a time/memory limit. On the36 fully timed
  cases, descriptive summed times were2.190s original versus1.045s proposed.
  These sums do not represent workload-frequency weighting. Small free cases
  acquired about5–28microseconds (6–14%) overhead. The rule is not always faster.
  At arbitrary precision it deliberately misses some small speedups; inspect
  analysis.json rather than assuming a perfect classifier.

Recommendation
  Review this calibrated two-route policy before implementation. Keep
  repeatedPermanent for profitable bunching and numerical stability. Replace
  the present term-count-only choice with a cost model which includes modes,
  occupied rows and arithmetic type. Recalibrate the empirical constants on
  another WL version/architecture. This artifact proposes a rule; it does not
  justify universal hardware-independent thresholds or an always-fastest claim.
