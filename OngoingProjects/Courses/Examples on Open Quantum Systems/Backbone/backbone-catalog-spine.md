# Backbone: What the Twenty Examples Share, and the Cost of Discarding the Record

This page is the statement layer of the catalog: a graph of assumptions, choices, and derived statements, each given in words and formulas only. Every node names its parents, so the dependency graph is recoverable from the page, and the layers order the reading from the setup to the headline and then to what holds when an assumption is weakened. Three conventions hold throughout. The page has two layers: a construction layer, where the physical roots are translated into the equation S0 by cited standard theory, and an analysis layer, where every consequence consumes S0 as a contract and therefore survives any construction that yields the same equation; the equation is never a root here, because one weakening below (collect only part of the record, open an unwatched channel) reaches beneath it. Every assumption must be consumed by at least one statement that is not its own weakening; a root failing that test is not an assumption of the theory but only a direction along which the model can be varied. And every statement's tail separates provenance (derived in place, cited, or conjectured) from verification (exact decision, analytic cross-check, numerical check, or none), naming the clause scope and what the checking route shares with the finding route. Node labels: R is a root, a setup assumption; C is a choice, a meaning we fixed rather than a fact; S is a derived statement, with S0 reserved for the equation of motion (E would name it if posited as a contract without its construction, which this page does not do); H is the headline, the terminal statement; N is a recorded non-assumption; a prime marks the same statement under a weakened root. Nothing here executes; the computations live downstream, in *open-system-simulation-catalog.md* (this folder), whose toolkit builds each object and whose twenty examples work them one at a time. This is the spine those examples share, not a twenty-first example: each individual example carries, or is owed, its own backbone, three of which (cavity cat, rapid purification, feedback cooling) sit beside this one. A two-routes companion in the style of *two-examples-from-minimal-assumptions.md* is owed but not yet written; the structural identities below are the ones the catalog's own toolkit cells already confirm. We set $\hbar = 1$.

## The setup, and the choice

**R1 (assumption).** The system, on any finite-dimensional Hilbert space, couples to a memoryless environment through a fixed list of channel operators $\{\hat c_k\}$: the environment's correlation time lies far below every system timescale, so the record-averaged evolution is time-local with constant rates. The dimension and the Hamiltonian are deliberately left open, so one construction serves a qubit, a truncated oscillator, or a joint atom-cavity. R1 without R2 is a real regime, the record thrown away, and is the whole of the catalog's Parts One through Three.

**R2 (assumption).** A detector is placed on the output and read continuously, and the record is kept: each monitored channel is unravelled either diffusively (homodyne or heterodyne, the output a noisy current $dJ = \langle\hat c + \hat c^\dagger\rangle\,dt + dW$) or by jumps (photon counting, discrete clicks $dN$), and each watched channel carries a detection efficiency $\eta_k$. The unravelling is a property of the detector, not of the system. This is the decision to keep the record rather than average it away, and it is separable from R1: R1 without R2 is the unconditioned master equation, R2 without R1 is a detector reading a system with a structured, non-Markovian bath.

**R3 (assumption).** The system starts in a pure state. This is load-bearing for the purity headline and for nothing else; the weakening S2″ keeps R4 and drops R3.

**R4 (assumption).** The record is complete and ideal: every channel that carries information away is monitored, at unit efficiency ($\eta_k = 1$), with no unmonitored leak and no extra unrecorded noise. This is a closure claim, a different kind from R2's existence claim: R2 asserts a detector sits on the output and reads it diffusively or by jumps, R4 asserts the list of information sinks ends at those detectors, and closure never follows from existence. The model of S2′, which keeps R2 and weakens R4, separates the two.

**C1 (choice).** What "the averaged dynamics" and "decoherence" mean. Averaging the conditioned state over the record is exactly tracing out the detector, so the record-averaged conditioned state is the master-equation state, $\mathbb E[\rho_c(t)] = \rho(t)$; and a monitored observable is scaled as $\hat c = \sqrt{k}\,\hat M$ with record $dJ = 2\sqrt{k}\,\langle\hat M\rangle\,dt + dW$, the catalog's convention. The rival number is the conditioned state itself, kept rather than averaged. The choice is load-bearing, not cosmetic: the purity of the average and the purity of each conditioned run are different numbers, and the headline is their disagreement. *Derived in place (averaging over the innovation erases the stochastic term, leaving the Lindbladian). Verified by numerical check, whole node: the toolkit's ensemble mean against the master equation, the two routes sharing no evolution code.*

## The equation, derived from the setup

**S0 (from R1, R2; cited).** Open-system theory turns the setup into a pair, the record-averaged Lindblad master equation
$$\dot\rho = \mathcal L\rho = -i[\hat H,\rho] + \sum_k \mathcal D[\hat c_k]\rho, \qquad \mathcal D[\hat c]\rho = \hat c\rho\hat c^\dagger - \tfrac12\{\hat c^\dagger\hat c,\rho\},$$
and its unravelling, the conditioned stochastic master equation, diffusive form
$$d\rho_c = \mathcal L\rho_c\,dt + \sum_k \sqrt{\eta_k}\,\mathcal H[\hat c_k]\rho_c\,dW_k, \qquad \mathcal H[\hat c]\rho = \hat c\rho + \rho\hat c^\dagger - \langle\hat c + \hat c^\dagger\rangle\rho,$$
with the jump superoperator $\mathcal G[\hat c]\rho = \hat c\rho\hat c^\dagger/\mathrm{Tr}[\hat c\rho\hat c^\dagger] - \rho$ replacing $\mathcal H$ at click times on a counted channel. Each root is visible in the shape: R1 is the Lindblad form with constant rates and the operator list $\{\hat c_k\}$; R2 is the added innovation term, diffusive $\mathcal H$ or jump $\mathcal G$, driven by the record; R4 is $\eta_k = 1$ on every channel with no generator term outside the watched list. *Cited: Wiseman and Milburn, Quantum Measurement and Control (Cambridge 2010); Breuer and Petruccione, The Theory of Open Quantum Systems (Oxford 2002), chapter 3; the positivity-preserving filter used for the simulation, Rouchon and Ralph (arXiv:1410.5345). Verification: none; the translation is trusted to its sources, and the catalog computes with exactly these equations.*

## Consequences

**S1 (from S0).** Every unravelling averages to the one master equation. Whichever detector is chosen, a photon counter, a homodyne receiver, a heterodyne one, or any member of the general-dyne family between them, the record-average of the conditioned state solves the master equation, $\mathbb E[\rho_c(t)] = \rho(t)$, because the innovation term is trace-free and has zero mean under the physical record measure. The single history depends on the detector; the average does not. This holds exactly in the continuum; the finite-step ensemble approaches it in a joint limit, the gap splitting into Monte-Carlo scatter that shrinks as $1/\sqrt N$ and a discretization bias that shrinks only as $dt \to 0$, and, the master equation being contractive, the surviving gap at a given time is smaller than the running sum of per-step biases. *Derived in place (the zero-mean innovation), from S0. Verified by numerical check, whole node: the toolkit's `gapDiagnosis` splitting sampling scatter from step bias in one run, applied to the diffusive ensembles and, in its own idiom, to the jump ones; no shared evolution code.*

**S2 (from S0, R3, R4).** The purity split. Under a complete ideal record (R4) of a pure start (R3), each conditioned trajectory stays pure for all time, because at a pure $\rho_c$ both the drift and the noise of the purity vanish: nothing is being discarded. The record-averaged state, meanwhile, is mixed. So the whole of decoherence is the purity of the average, the cost of discarding the record, and is not an event in any single watched history. *Derived in place (the purity increment vanishing at $\rho_c$ pure, read from the impurity law $d\ell = -4k\ell(\sin^2\theta + 2\ell\cos^2\theta)\,dt - 4\sqrt k\,\ell\sqrt{1-2\ell}\cos\theta\,dW$, both terms carrying $\ell$); cited: Wiseman and Milburn (2010). Verified by numerical check, whole node: one trajectory ending pure while the ensemble mean ends mixed, and the mean of the conditioned purities held at one against the purity of the mean falling to its mixed value; no shared code.*

## The headline

**H (from S1, S2, C1).** Conditionality is the spine of the catalog. A complete ideal record keeps an initially pure state on a pure stochastic trajectory; decoherence is the cost of discarding the record, not something that happens to any watched state; and every way of watching one leak unravels the same master equation into genuinely different single histories. Mechanism, in words: the environment carries which-outcome information away, and a detector that captures all of it leaves the conditioned state pure, so the mixing seen in the average is bookkeeping over unread outcomes rather than a physical process in the system; choosing the detector sets what is learned moment to moment while leaving the unconditioned average untouched. Audit: H rests on the contract S0 (the master equation and its unravelling) together with R3, R4, and C1; S0 is realized from R1 and R2 by the cited translation, so the analysis survives any construction that yields the same equations. No conjecture anywhere in the closure. *Derived in place from S1 and S2. Verified by numerical check, whole node, through S1's averaging identity and S2's purity split; no shared code.*

## When an assumption is weakened

**S2′ (from R4 weakened; S0).** Relax completeness, by a detection efficiency $\eta_k < 1$, an unmonitored channel, a thermal drive, or extra dephasing, and trajectory theory changes the equation beneath: the readout coefficient becomes $\sqrt{\eta_k k}$ while the disturbance keeps its full $k$, or an unwatched dissipator is added to $\mathcal L$. The conditioned state itself now mixes, and the catalog reads the loss as a floor: the impurity floor $(1-\eta)/2$ in rapid purification, the phonon-number floor in feedback cooling, and the mixed fluorescence-only run against the pure all-channels run in the driven, watched atom. Missed information mixes the conditioned state; catching all of it is what kept it pure. This is the weakening that reaches beneath the equation, which is why the page owes its construction layer rather than positing the contract alone. *Derived in place (the efficiency on the readout against the fixed back-action; an added channel's own attractor); cited: Wiseman and Ralph 2006 (quant-ph/0603062), Doherty and Jacobs 1999 (Phys. Rev. A 60, 2700). Verified by numerical check, by clause and each at a single setting, in the catalog's rapid-purification, feedback-cooling, and driven-atom sections; other settings untested.*

**S2″ (from R3 weakened; S0).** Start mixed instead. Under an ideal diffusive record the conditioned state purifies toward one as the record accumulates, so the pure trajectory is the attractor, not only the pure-start special case: the localization runs collapse a biased mixed start onto a $\hat\sigma_z$ eigenstate, and the Kalman covariance is squeezed to the Heisenberg floor whatever its broad start. The headline's "stays pure" is the pure-start reading; "purifies to pure" is the mixed-start one. *Derived in place (the conditioned purity's non-negative drift under efficient monitoring), from S0; cited: Wiseman and Milburn (2010). Verified by numerical check: the purification and localization curves from the maximally mixed and biased starts; no shared code.*

**N (a recorded non-assumption).** Nothing on this page rests on the system being a qubit or a single mode, or on the master equation having a unique steady state. The channel list $\{\hat c_k\}$, the unravelling, and the averaging identity are stated for any finite Hilbert space and any number of steady directions; the twenty examples run the one toolkit over a qubit, a truncated oscillator, a joint atom-cavity, and a Gaussian moment bookkeeping without touching the spine. The boundary of the deletion: the closed forms each example leans on, Bloch equations, coherent-state dyads, Gaussian moments, are per-example conveniences, and the pure-dephasing bath's whole line of steady states shows that the unique-steady-state reading is genuinely optional. *Derived in place (the construction never reads the dimension or the steady-state count). Verified by numerical check: one trajectory-and-averaging route driving every example across dimensions.*

## The abstract, read off the graph

A memoryless environment carries information out of a system through a fixed list of channel operators, and the record-averaged evolution is the Lindblad master equation. Put a detector on the output and keep the record, and that one master equation unravels into single histories, differently for a photon counter, a homodyne receiver, or a heterodyne one, yet every unravelling averages back to the same master equation, exactly in the continuum and in a joint many-trajectory, small-step limit in simulation. When the record is complete and ideal and the state starts pure, each conditioned history stays pure while only the record-blind average mixes, so decoherence is the cost of discarding the record, not an event in any watched state, and the measurement chosen sets what is learned moment to moment without touching the average. Weaken the completeness, by inefficiency, an unwatched channel, a thermal drive, or dephasing, and the conditioned state itself mixes, reading out as the impurity and phonon-number floors the catalog meets again and again; start mixed instead and the conditioned state purifies toward the same pure trajectory. None of this reads the dimension of the system or assumes a unique steady state, which is why one small toolkit carries all twenty examples.
