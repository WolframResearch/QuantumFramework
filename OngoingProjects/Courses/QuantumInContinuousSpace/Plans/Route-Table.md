## C1 verdict

CLASS: C1 exactly solvable stationary ODE
REPRESENTATIVE: 12.1 [BSc] "How do I solve the Coulomb radial equation and obtain the bound-state energies $E_n=-1/(2n^2)$?": $-\tfrac12 u'' + (\tfrac{l(l+1)}{2r^2} - \tfrac1r)u = E u$ on $(0,\infty)$, quantization from normalizability at both ends.
CLASSIFICATION: linear, homogeneous, second-order ODE eigenproblems (singular Sturm-Liouville); representative has a regular singular point at $r=0$, irregular at $\infty$, discrete negative point spectrum accumulating at $0^-$ plus a positive continuum; members span pure point on $\mathbb{R}$ (Hermite), Floquet band spectrum (Mathieu), and scattering continuum (Coulomb waves).
ROUTES ENUMERATED:
- R1 `DSolve` with symbolic $E$: general solution in special functions; quantization imposed manually (termination/pole condition), then `Solve` for $E_n$; residual + norm verification.
- R2 `DEigensystem` domain form: exact $\{\lambda_n,\psi_n\}$ where it evaluates.
- R3 Transform (`LaplaceTransform` of the radial equation): admissible in principle, unprobed, redundant given R1 succeeds.
- R4 `NDEigensystem`/`NDEigenvalues` on a truncated domain, Arnoldi + shift: numeric spectrum.
- R5 `NDSolve` shooting on the decay condition: alternative numeric route, unprobed, less falsifiable than R4 (root-finding can silently skip levels).
- R6 `SchrodingerPDEComponent` assembly then R4: builds the same 1D operator, adds nothing symbolic for this class.
CRITERION: exactness > falsifiability > independence > inspectability > cost (stated before scoring).
PRIMARY ROUTE: R1. `DSolve` the ODE with symbolic $E$ (returns `WhittakerM`/`WhittakerW`, index $\kappa=-i/\sqrt{2E}$; for fixed $l$, `Hypergeometric1F1`/`HypergeometricU`); select the normalizable branch by hand; quantization is the manual series-termination read-off $1/\sqrt{-2E}=n$, `Solve` giving $E_n=-1/(2n^2)$; eigenfunctions $u_{n,l}=r^{l+1}e^{-r/n}L_{n-l-1}^{2l+1}(2r/n)$ via `LaguerreL` (members: `HermiteH`, `MathieuCharacteristicA/B`+`MathieuC/S`, `CoulombF/G`); verify by `FullSimplify` residual $\to 0$ and exact `Integrate` norms.
CROSS-CHECK ROUTE: R4 `NDEigenvalues` on $[\varepsilon,R]$ with `DirichletCondition`, `Method -> {"Eigensystem" -> {"Arnoldi", "Shift" -> below ground}}`, sweeping BOTH `MaxCellMeasure` and $\varepsilon$; independent machinery (FEM vs special-function algebra). For members on $\mathbb{R}$, R2 domain form is a free exact second confirmation; for 9.1, FEM with `PeriodicBoundaryCondition` on one cell.

## C4 verdict

CLASS: C4 fixed-energy scattering BVP
REPRESENTATIVE: 6.2 [BSc] "How do I compute the tunneling transmission $T(E)$ through a rectangular barrier?"
CLASSIFICATION: linear homogeneous second-order ODE (stationary 1D Schrodinger at fixed $E$ in the continuum); piecewise-constant coefficients for members 6.x/9.x, variable (centrifugal $l(l+1)/r^2$) coefficient for 20.x, first-order two-component system for 22.5.
