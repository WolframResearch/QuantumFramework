# Related papers: what experimentalists need from measurement-in-a-potential-class models

Collected 2026-08-06 for `measurement-in-a-potential.md`. Layout mirrors the Aspelmeyer corpus: `eprint/<id>.tar.gz` (original arXiv e-prints) and `tex/<id>/` (extracted sources). All main texts were read in full on collection day; supplementary TeX files are extracted here for future use. Three closely related eprints already live in `../../Aspelmeyer/eprint/` and are not duplicated: 2410.17253 (FLIP), 2508.10601 (double-well stabilization), 2509.17503 (free expansion via electrostatic compensation), plus the two-mass entanglement theory pair 2408.06251 / 2408.07492.

## Release, expansion, and recapture (macroscopic-superposition program)
- **2408.01264** Rossi, Militaru, Carlon Zambon, Riera-Campeny, Romero-Isart, Frimmer, Novotny. *Quantum Delocalization of a Levitated Nanoparticle.* Experiment (ETH). Frequency-jump expansion of a ground-state-cooled particle; coherence length 73 pm > ground state via momentum squeezing (-7.2 dB); state read out by an SME-based retrodiction filter; estimation noise V_n = 1.1 vs 0.5 ideal (weak measurement = simultaneous x,p measurement); positive-semidefinite covariance subtraction.
- **2312.13111** Bonvin et al. (ETH + Innsbruck). *State Expansion in a Dark Harmonic Potential.* Experiment. x25 thermal-state expansion in a hybrid Paul-optical trap; full Mathieu/Floquet + stochastic-heating model; micromotion nearly doubles expansion for stiff traps; heating amplified by r^2.
- **2512.11633** Seta, Devaud, Dania, Novotny, Frimmer. *Shot-to-shot displacement noise in state-expansion protocols with inverted potentials.* Experiment + model. Identifies a quenched noise class (shot-to-shot potential misalignment, Eq. for sigma_shot) that white-noise models miss; requirement sigma_disp < xi_0/50 (~0.5 pm) for 1 nm coherence length; suggests per-shot estimation and correction.
- **2507.12995** Mattana et al. (ETH). *Trap-to-trap free falls.* Experiment. Charge-neutral particle, 0.25 ms free fall, ~200x position-uncertainty growth at recapture.
- **2509.17503** (in Aspelmeyer corpus) Steiner, Fein, et al. (Vienna). *Free expansion via electrostatic compensation.* Experiment. 3D stray-force compensation (cross-talk matrix method), 100 us free evolutions, 56x expansion; covariance evolution via differential Lyapunov equation.

## Squeezing below zero point and time-of-flight state reconstruction
- **2504.17944** Kamba, Hara, Aikawa (Tokyo). *Quantum squeezing of a levitated nanomechanical oscillator.* Experiment (Science 389, 1225 (2025)). Velocity variance -4.9 dB below ground state via rapid frequency jumps; verified by TOF free expansion; full technical-noise budget (V_n = 0.21); spinning particle (GHz) to gyroscopically decouple librations.
- **2605.09854** Otabe, Kamba, Kojima, Aikawa. *TOF force sensing below the quantum zero-point fluctuation.* Experiment. 10-zN static force sensing with squeezed velocity; TOF quantum state tomography (300 phases x 600 shots, MLE) reconstructing the Wigner function; Fisher-information sensitivity analysis.
- **2306.16598** Kamba, Aikawa. *Velocity uncertainties in the quantum ground state.* Experiment. Release-and-recapture TOF of a ground-state particle; librations broaden velocity distributions via an atomic-scale offset (0.2 nm) between libration center and center of mass; rigid-body model.

## Nonlinear and inverted potentials under feedback (dark levitation)
- **2410.17253** (Aspelmeyer corpus) Dago et al. *FLIP: feedback levitation on an inverted potential.* Experiment. Kalman-LQG holds particle at double-well apex (sigma_x ~ 9 nm); adaptive filter co-estimates the drifting apex position as an extra state.
- **2508.10601** (Aspelmeyer corpus) Mlynar, Dago, et al. *Feedback stabilization at the intensity minimum of a double-well.* Control-theory paper + experiment. Augmented-state Kalman (x, x', apex, z, z') with random-walk parameter model and constrained (projected) estimation; FPGA LQG; notes LQG extends to the quantum regime (Belavkin/Edwards).
- **2606.10042** Grochowski, Romero-Isart. *Non-Gaussian state preparation via time-dependent control of weakly nonharmonic potentials.* Theory (PRX-style). Fock/cat/Bell states via optimal control of the linear term with static cubic nonharmonicity; delocalization as transient resource; noise model includes nonlinear dissipator D[x^2] (trap-curvature noise); their simulation engine is a split-step grid (2^10 points).

## Two particles, multimode, entanglement
- **2409.04863** Deplano, Pontin, Ranfagni, Marino, Marin (Florence). *High-purity 2D levitated oscillator.* Experiment. Strong-coupling 2D regime with spectral overlap; 6x6 Lyapunov covariance from heterodyne fits; purity 0.209 above independent-oscillator value; quantum discord; frame-independent indicators; explicit goal: 2D mechanical entanglement.
- **2502.08563** Pontin et al. (Florence). *Strong coupling and dark modes in a pair of levitated nanoparticles.* Experiment. Cavity-mediated coupling between two particles in a bichromatic tweezer; avoided crossings ~1 kHz; dark mode forms at bare-frequency crossing; Coulomb coupling included.
- **2505.12865** Cao, Guo, Tan. *Entangling two levitated particles in free space via trap modulation and Bayesian feedback.* Theory. 4-mode conditional Riccati + Kalman + LQR; frequency modulation omega(t) = omega_m[1+alpha cos(Omega t)]^2 squeezes both normal modes; Coulomb coupling differentiates them -> log-negativity; unconditional entanglement at eta ~ 0.5, g/omega_m ~ 0.1.
- **2406.04361** Miki, Matsumura, Yamamoto. *Feasible generation of gravity-induced entanglement using optomechanical systems.* Theory (PRD 110, 024057). GIE between mirrors under continuous measurement + Kalman filtering; time evolution from the Riccati equation; entanglement impossible without optimal filtering; error propagation from covariance uncertainty to Delta E_N and S/N.

## Rotors
- **2509.13398** Troyer, Fechtel, et al. (Vienna/Duisburg, Nat. Phys. 2026). *Ground-state cooling of two librational modes of a nanorotor.* Experiment. n_alpha = 0.21 (1D); two modes simultaneously near GS; orientation fixed to ~17 urad; outlook: release -> angular-momentum superpositions, revivals T_rev = 2 pi I / hbar (50 min; 300 ms for 10 MDa), fractional revivals; dark (laser-free) cooling needed for bio-nanorotors.

## Gravity and collapse tests
- **2605.28479** Uitenbroek, Langendorff, et al. (Leiden). *Picometer control of a levitating milligram gravity sensor.* Experiment. 0.35 mg magnet in superconducting trap; two modes feedback-cooled to ~7 mK (~10^6 phonons) at 50-68 Hz, 1.2-1.6 pm RMS; roadmap to GS (quantum-limited SQUID, feedback on isolation-stage modes); same platform as the mg-scale gravity measurement.
- **2501.08971** Altamura, Vinante, Carlesso. *Improved CSL bounds from LISA Pathfinder rotational noise.* Data analysis (PRA Letters). Torque-noise bound beats translational one; general criterion (alpha ratio) for when rotational DOF constrain collapse models better; cube geometry eta_R, eta_V.
