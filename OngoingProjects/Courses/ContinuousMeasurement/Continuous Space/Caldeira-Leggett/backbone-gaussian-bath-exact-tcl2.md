# Backbone: When the Exact Gaussian Generator Stops at Second Order

This page is the statement layer of the draft [*When Does the Exact Gaussian Generator Stop at Second Order?*](gaussian-bath-exact-tcl2-conditions.md), extracted from that draft alone: a graph of assumptions, choices, and derived statements, each given in words and formulas only. Every node names its parents, so the dependency graph is recoverable from the page. The page has two layers: a construction layer, where physical roots are translated into the equation node S0 by cited theory, and an analysis layer, where every consequence consumes S0 as a contract and therefore survives any construction that yields the same objects. One structural note is owed at the top, because the draft's subject is itself a construction: the draft turns a microscopic model into a master equation, so the equation slot S0 here holds not one master equation but the exact reduced map, its time-local generator, and their recursion, imported whole from the companion construction paper [*Exact Dyson Maps and Time-Local Master Equations for a Thermal Oscillator Bath*](gaussian-bath-exact-dyson-map.md); the master equation the title asks about emerges downstream, inside the headline, as a consequence of the contract. Every assumption is consumed by at least one statement that is not its own weakening. Every statement's tail separates provenance (derived in place, cited, or conjectured) from verification (exact decision, analytic cross-check, numerical check, or none), naming the clause scope. The verifications cited in the tails live in the draft's own closing section of mathematical qualifications and checks; there is no separate computation file. No primed nodes appear, because the draft computes no weakened roots: its only turning-off computation removes the hypothesis of S2 rather than a root, and it is recorded in S1's tail. ## The setup, and the choice

**R1 (assumption).** Product preparation: at the preparation time $t=0$ the joint state is $\hat\rho_{SB}(0)=\hat\rho_S(0)\otimes\hat\rho_B(0)$. Every bath trace then acts on a freely chosen initial system operator, and the reduced Dyson series carries no initial-correlation terms. A correlated preparation over the same bath keeps R2 and drops R1.

**R2 (assumption).** The bath is a collection of independent harmonic oscillators, initially thermal at inverse temperature $\beta$, exerting the force $\hat B=\sum_j c_j\hat q_j$, linear in the oscillator coordinates. What the analysis consumes is one consequence of this: every odd ordered bath-force moment vanishes, and every even ordered moment is a sum over pairings of the two-point correlation $C(t-s)=\operatorname{Tr}_B[\hat B_I(t)\hat B_I(s)\hat\rho_B(0)]$, with $C(-\tau)=C(\tau)^*$. This pairing property is the only meaning "Gaussian bath" carries on this page; no claim of Gaussian reduced system dynamics is made anywhere. An anharmonic or mode-coupled bath keeps R1 and R3 and drops R2.

**R3 (assumption).** One Hermitian coupling channel, $\hat H_I=-\hat X\otimes\hat B$, in the interaction picture generated by $\hat H_S+\hat H_B$, with $\hat H_S$ including the companion paper's counterterm and held fixed when counting interaction orders. Every interaction event therefore inserts one factor of the single orbit $\hat X_I(t)=e^{i\hat H_St/\hbar}\hat Xe^{-i\hat H_St/\hbar}$, so the system side of every pairing is a string of orbit values at ordered times. Several coupling channels keep R1 and R2 and drop R3.

**R4 (assumption, a scope restriction).** The system Hilbert space is finite dimensional and $\hat H_S$ is time independent. Consumed only by the equivalence S3; the exactness statements S0 through S2 never use it.

**R5 (assumption, analysis-domain regularity).** The correlation $C$ and the ordered time integrals built from it are finite, or an explicit regulator is retained; the Dyson series, when treated as an equality, converges on the operator domain considered; and $\Phi_t$ is invertible at the times where the generator is written. The boundary: the Dyson coefficients themselves need none of the invertibility clause, and under the commuting condition of S2 in finite dimension the map is a finite matrix exponential, invertible whenever $\mathcal M_2(t)$ is finite.

**C1 (choice).** "Exact at second order" means the coupling expansion of the exact time-local generator terminates: $\mathcal L_{2m}(t)=0$ for every $m\geq2$, with every power of the bookkeeping parameter summed, so this is not a weak-coupling truncation. It is distinct from the exactness of the map $\Phi_t$, which holds at every order by construction; from an exact solution for one particular initial state or one selected bath; and from the vanishing of higher bath cumulants, which is a property of the bath (R2), not of the generator. Time locality is also weaker than Markovianity: the generator below is time local at every order whether or not it terminates. *Fixed in place from the draft's Sections 2, 4, and 7. Verification: the separation of the readings is witnessed by S1's noncommuting-qubit check, where the bath is Gaussian yet the fourth-order generator is nonzero.*

## The equation, imported as the contract

**S0 (from R1, R2, R3, R5; cited).** The exact reduced dynamics of the companion construction. With $\lambda$ recording interaction order ($\lambda=1$ restores the model),

$$
\Phi_t=\mathcal I_S+\sum_{m\geq1}\lambda^{2m}\mathcal M_{2m}(t),
\qquad
\hat\rho_{S,I}(t)=\Phi_t[\hat\rho_S(0)],
\qquad
\mathcal L_I(t)=\dot\Phi_t\Phi_t^{-1}=\sum_{m\geq1}\lambda^{2m}\mathcal L_{2m}(t),
$$

with the recursion

$$
\mathcal L_{2m}(t)=\dot{\mathcal M}_{2m}(t)-\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}\mathcal L_{2k}(t)\,\mathcal M_{2(m-k)}(t),
$$

and every $\mathcal M_{2m}$ an ordered time integral of bath-traced nested commutators whose scalar coefficients are products of $C$. The second-order integrand, abbreviated $\mathcal A_2(t,s)=-\mathcal K_2(t,s;\cdot)/\hbar^2$, is

$$
\mathcal K_2(t,s;\hat\rho)=C(t-s)\left[\hat X_I(t)\hat X_I(s)\hat\rho-\hat X_I(s)\hat\rho\hat X_I(t)\right]+C(s-t)\left[\hat\rho\hat X_I(s)\hat X_I(t)-\hat X_I(t)\hat\rho\hat X_I(s)\right].
$$

Each root is visible in the shape: R1 makes the series act on $\hat\rho_S(0)$ alone; R2 leaves only even orders, with coefficients paired into $C$; R3 makes every operator in every string the one orbit $\hat X_I$; R5 licenses the inverse defining $\mathcal L_I$. *Cited: the companion paper (its Appendix A for the vanishing odd moments, the pairing rule, and the maps; its exact-closure section for $\Phi_t$, the generator, and the recursion); the recursion also Nestmann and Timm, arXiv:1903.05132; the Gaussian-map structure also Diósi and Ferialdi, arXiv:1408.1273, and Ferialdi, arXiv:1512.07244. Verification, by clause: dimensional consistency, the decoupling limit $C=0$ giving $\Phi_t=\mathcal I_S$, and trace preservation of the generator are recorded in the draft's checks section; the construction is otherwise trusted to its sources, and every check below computes with exactly these maps.*

## Consequences

**S1 (from S0).** The fourth-order generator is an ordering difference and nothing else. Differentiating the recursion at fourth order and splitting the domain of the composition $\dot{\mathcal M}_2\mathcal M_2$ over the three possible positions of its single integration time relative to the ordered pair gives

$$
\mathcal L_4(t)=\int_0^t dt_1\int_0^{t_1}dt_2\int_0^{t_2}dt_3\sum_{\text{three pairings}}\Bigl(\mathcal T[\mathcal A_2\,\mathcal A_2]-\mathcal A_2\,\mathcal A_2\Bigr),
$$

where the sum runs over the pairings $(t,t_1)(t_2,t_3)$, $(t,t_2)(t_1,t_3)$, $(t,t_3)(t_1,t_2)$ of the four ordered times, and $\mathcal T$ restores the chronological order of the individual $\hat X_I$ factors inside the expanded product, without treating either $\mathcal A_2$ as indivisible. The same three pairings appear in both terms: Wick pairing removes every independent higher bath correlation, and what survives is exactly the chronological ordering of noncommuting system operators. *Derived in place (the draft's Eqs. (12) through (17)). Verified by numerical check, whole node: the sixteen left-or-right terms of each pairing expanded for two qubit models; the commuting coupling gave a difference vanishing at floating-point precision, and the orbit generated by $\hat H_S\propto\hat\sigma_z$ with $\hat X=\hat\sigma_x$ gave a nonzero difference; the draft records the two outcomes and no parameter sweep, so these are single-configuration checks.*

**S2 (from S0, S1).** If $[\hat X_I(t),\hat X_I(s)]=0$ for all $s$ and $t$, chronological reordering changes no paired product: $\mathcal L_4=0$, and at every order the $m!$ permutations of the $m$ labeled pair insertions represent the same Dyson contribution, giving

$$
\mathcal M_{2m}(t)=\frac{\mathcal M_2(t)^m}{m!},
\qquad
\Phi_t=e^{\lambda^2\mathcal M_2(t)},
\qquad
\mathcal L_I(t)=\lambda^2\dot{\mathcal M}_2(t),
\qquad
\mathcal L_{2m}(t)=0\ \ (m\geq2).
$$

The last step uses $[\mathcal M_2,\dot{\mathcal M}_2]=0$, immediate once all left and right multiplications by orbit values commute. Every power of $\lambda$ has been summed; the termination is exact, not perturbative. *Derived in place: the fourth order by the full algebra of S1, the all-order case by the pairing-permutation counting argument the draft gives in words. Verification, by clause: the fourth-order clause through S1's commuting-qubit check; the exponential form and orders six and higher carry no check beyond the displayed algebra.*

**S3 (from R3, R4).** In finite dimension with time-independent $\hat H_S$, the commuting orbit is exactly pure dephasing: $[\hat X_I(t),\hat X_I(s)]=0$ for all times if and only if $[\hat H_S,\hat X]=0$. Forward: the orbit is constant, $\hat X_I(t)=\hat X$. Converse: differentiating $[\hat X_I(t),\hat X]=0$ at $t=0$ gives $[[\hat H_S,\hat X],\hat X]=0$, whose matrix elements in an eigenbasis of $\hat X$ read

$$
(x_b-x_a)^2\,\langle a|\hat H_S|b\rangle=0,
$$

so $\hat H_S$ connects no two eigenspaces of $\hat X$ carrying different eigenvalues; degenerate eigenspaces are harmless because $\hat X$ is a multiple of the identity inside each. The coupling then causes no transitions between system energies and touches only relative phases. *Derived in place; the algebra is displayed and checkable by eye. Verification: none beyond it.*

## The headline

**H (from C1, S2, S3).** Pure dephasing stops the exact Gaussian generator at second order:

$$
[\hat H_S,\hat X]=0
\ \Longrightarrow\
[\hat X_I(t),\hat X_I(s)]=0
\ \Longrightarrow\
\mathcal M_{2m}=\frac{\mathcal M_2^m}{m!}
\ \Longrightarrow\
\mathcal L_{2m}=0\quad(m\geq2),
$$

and the resulting master equation, exact to all coupling strengths for which the map exists in the sense fixed by C1, is the familiar pure-dephasing one,

$$
\frac{d\hat\rho_{S,I}(t)}{dt}=-\frac{\lambda^2}{\hbar^2}\int_0^t d\tau\left\{\operatorname{Re}C(\tau)\,[\hat X,[\hat X,\hat\rho_{S,I}(t)]]+i\operatorname{Im}C(\tau)\,[\hat X^2,\hat\rho_{S,I}(t)]\right\}:
$$

in the common eigenbasis of $\hat H_S$ and $\hat X$ the populations stay constant, the real part of $C$ shrinks each coherence through the accumulated $(x_a-x_b)^2$ weight, and the imaginary part turns its phase through a Hamiltonian correction proportional to $\hat X^2$, absent whenever $\hat X^2$ is a multiple of the identity, as for a Pauli coupling. Mechanism, in words: pairwise bath statistics never close the generator by themselves, because what every higher coefficient measures is the chronological ordering of noncommuting system operators; pure dephasing is the condition that makes that ordering invisible, at fourth order and at every order above it. The converse is open: it has not been shown that universal termination, meaning $\mathcal L_{2m}(t)=0$ for every $m\geq2$, every time, and every physical correlation $C$, forces $[\hat H_S,\hat X]=0$. A nonzero integrand can integrate to zero for a particular correlation or a particular final time, so necessity requires the family of physical thermal correlations to be rich enough to block every such cancellation, and if it is not, an explicit counterexample without pure dephasing must exist. Audit: H rests on the contract S0 together with C1, through S1 and S2, and on S3; S0 is realized from R1, R2, R3 by the cited companion construction, R5 underwrites the existence and invertibility of its objects, and R4 enters only through the equivalence S3. No node in the closure is conjectured; the one open statement, necessity, enters no node and is recorded as open in this gloss. *Derived in place from S2 and S3 by substituting the second-order integrand; the closure itself is known and cited: Doll, Zueco, Wubs, Kohler, and Hänggi, arXiv:0707.3938. Verification: through the tails of S1 and S2, plus the draft's recorded direct check of the closed equation against the second-order integrand; that checking route shares the integrand's definition with the derivation.*

## When an assumption is weakened

The draft computes no weakened roots, so this page records no primed variants: the corner models named in R1, R2, and R3 are uncomputed directions, and the draft's only contrast computation removes the hypothesis of S2 rather than a root (the noncommuting qubit orbit in S1's check, where the fourth-order difference is nonzero).

**N1 (a recorded non-assumption).** No Born, Markov, secular, rotating-wave, high-temperature, or Ohmic-spectrum approximation appears anywhere above, on purpose: the termination in S2 is an all-order cancellation, not a truncation, so nothing rests on any of these approximations. The boundary of the deletion: the regularity those approximations would have imposed must instead be assumed explicitly, which is exactly the content of R5. *Stated in place by the draft; no statement above consumes any of these approximations, and that detachment is the content.*

## The abstract, read off the graph

A system coupled through one Hermitian channel to independent thermal oscillators, prepared as a product, has an exact reduced dynamics determined by the two-point bath correlation alone: odd bath moments vanish, even ones split into pairings, and the exact reduced map, its time-local generator, and their recursion exist order by order with coefficients built from $C$. That pairing does not by itself close the generator at second order, because the paired correlations multiply system operators whose interaction-picture values need not commute at different times: the entire fourth-order generator is the difference between chronologically ordering those operators and composing two second-order maps. If the orbit commutes at all pairs of times, the difference vanishes at every order, the map is the exponential of its second-order term, and the generator is exactly the second-order one at every coupling for which the map exists. In finite dimension with a time-independent system Hamiltonian, the commuting orbit is precisely pure dephasing, and the resulting master equation holds populations fixed while the real part of the correlation shrinks coherences and its imaginary part turns their phases. Sufficiency is proved; whether pure dephasing is also necessary for termination under every physical correlation remains open.
