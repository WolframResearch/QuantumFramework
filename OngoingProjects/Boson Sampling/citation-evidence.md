Fresh-context check (2026-09-27): a reviewer with no access to this table or to the essay judged 20 statements against the full texts: the essay's 14 citation sentences mixed with 6 planted false statements (Tillmann at four photons in six modes; the n² bound as proven for approximate sampling; the Tichy law as necessary; one permanent per Clifford sample; exact sampling implying P = NP; Spring's chip as fused fibre). It caught all six: five contradicted, and the sixth flagged as a platform swapped with Broome's. It supported all 14 essay sentences (HOM and Reck against their abstracts only) and raised two scope notes, both now in the essay: boson sampling is defined for a given interferometer, which the hardness argument chooses at random, and the approximate-hardness result is scoped to random interferometers with many modes.
