# Formal Semantics

A formal semantics of QuantumFramework in conventional quantum notation, organized around the one idea that QF represents states and transformations together with their bases and subsystem structure, and then uses that information to compose them, discard subsystems, and read out measurements: states and operators, what the basis carries (stored coefficients versus the represented map), maps on density matrices (the doubled representation behind channels, evolution, and phase space), combining objects (apply, compose, tensor, adjoint, partial trace), channels and measurements branch by branch, parameters and evolution, the phase-space picture, the stabilizer representation, the execution methods, distances and entanglement as functionals on the density matrix, and the assumptions and limitations. The appendices hold the exhaustive tables and the placement against the published literature, which is the only part that cites.

- `QF-formal-semantics.md`: the document, anchored to `main` 2d3c29e8 (2026-09-22, paclet 2.1.1); it names public functions, properties, and messages, never internal ones or line numbers.
- `return-shapes-check.wls`: the kernel run behind the table of return heads in section 4; it loads the working tree with `PacletDirectoryLoad`. `return-shapes-check.out.txt` is its output.
- `phase-space-functor-check.wls`: the run behind the phase-space transform of section 7 and Appendix C (phase-point operators, composition preservation, equivariance, the inverse, Cliffords as permutations, the SIC frame, the sign-sector transform, the rate matrix, a channel, the even-d convention). `phase-space-functor-check.out.txt` is its output; the messages in it are the witness of the `QuantumPositiveTransform` wrapper defect recorded in `implementation-mismatches.md`.
- `tableau-representation-check.wls`: the run behind the tableau representation of section 8 (the two storage shapes, the two conversions to and from the tensor representation, application through upvalues and through `Method -> "Stabilizer"`, measurement, `StabilizerFrame`, channels, graph states). `tableau-representation-check.out.txt` is its output.
- `section4-audit-check.wls`: the doubling and phase-space facts (`"Bend"` as the doubling, the shapes of the classical maps, the cup and the snake identity, the even-dimensional phase-space basis, the square-root inverse, the rate matrix and its column sums, the two transforms on a channel), the maps-on-density-matrices facts of section 3 (`"Left"`, `"Right"`, `"Liouvillian"`, and a channel's action), the measurement-versus-channel list constructors of section 5 (effects square-rooted to measurement operators versus Kraus operators kept), and the phase-space symbolic-time defect (item 8 of `implementation-mismatches.md`). `section4-audit-check.out.txt` is its output.
- `defect-repro.wls`: the `QuantumPositiveTransform` defect (item 1 of `implementation-mismatches.md`) and the two earlier problems now fixed, each as the failing call followed by the working call it should have matched. `defect-repro.out.txt` is its output.
- `section3-audit-check.wls`: the combining rules of section 4 (padding to the full input order, sorting before composition, mixed pictures, parameter intervals, the dagger and the partial trace as coordinate operations, the channel adjoint, the post-measurement state) and the circuit-and-operator defects, items 2 to 7 of `implementation-mismatches.md` (mixed pictures, the reversed cup and cap, the basis-mismatched trace, the matrix-type scalar sum, the channel-on-circuit compile, the repeated-wire trace). `section3-audit-check.out.txt` is its output.
- `derived-functionals-check.wls`: the facts of section 10 (the `QuantumDistance` default `"Fidelity"` measure as the distance `1 - Re Tr sqrt(rho_a rho_b)`, `QuantumSimilarity` as `1 - d`, the default `QuantumEntanglementMonotone` concurrence, and `QuantumEntangledQ`'s realignment criterion). `derived-functionals-check.out.txt` is its output.
- `evidence-status.md`: the claim-by-claim evidence record for the document, one status and check script per claim, kept beside the scripts rather than in the shared document; a point-in-time audit at `2d3c29e8`, not a certification.
- `implementation-mismatches.md`: the eight places where the kernel departs from the model (two earlier ones now fixed), moved out of the shared document; a point-in-time audit at `2d3c29e8`, and whether each is a defect or a design choice is a separate judgment.
